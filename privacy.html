Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 11, 2026 | Effective Date: September 11, 2026

Compliant with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDPA 2023) and Information Technology Act, 2000

1. Introduction & Scope

KrishiSetu ("we", "our", or "platform") operates as an agricultural decision-support and trade-verification platform designed to assist Indian farmers, Farmer Producer Organizations (FPOs), cold storage operators, and licensed agricultural commodity processors. We are committed to upholding the highest standards of data integrity, confidentiality, and farmer autonomy.

This Privacy Policy governs the collection, processing, storage, and protection of information submitted when utilizing the KrishiSetu decision engine, directory registries, and verification modules.

2. Information We Collect

We adhere strictly to the principle of data minimization. The categories of information processed include:

  • Harvest & Batch Metadata: Crop species, estimated harvest weight, perceived quality attributes, date of harvest, and expected storage shelf-life.
  • Geographical Data: Mandi district and state parameters strictly used for calculating freight distances, proximity to verified processing clusters, and localized market pricing.
  • Verification & Registry Records: Government-issued merchant credentials, including APMC license IDs, UDYAM registration numbers, and FSSAI certificates submitted for directory verification.
  • Technical Diagnostics: Browser environment, user language preference, and interface interaction timestamps required to maintain session security and prevent fraudulent access.

Note: KrishiSetu does not collect Aadhaar biometrics, financial credit card numbers, or proprietary land title deeds.

3. Purpose of Processing & Legal Basis

We process collected data exclusively for legitimate agricultural facilitation purposes:

  • Executing mathematical degradation curves to predict remaining shelf-life under ambient versus cold storage conditions.
  • Generating objective, rule-based recommendations across four distinct pathways: Sell, Store, Process, or Rescue.
  • Facilitating cryptographic verification of batch provenance via standardized QR-encoded digital batch certificates.
  • Preventing market fraud by verifying dealer and processor credentials against simulated regulatory rosters.

4. Prohibition of Data Monetization

Zero Third-Party Advertising: KrishiSetu does not sell, lease, rent, or trade farmer personal or operational data to data brokers, advertising networks, or speculative commodity trading desks.

Aggregated, strictly non-personally identifiable statistical summaries (such as regional tomato arrival volumes) may be analyzed solely to enhance agricultural research and logistics infrastructure planning.

5. Data Storage, Security & Retention

All data transmissions are secured using standard Transport Layer Security (TLS 1.3). Batch records stored within the browser utilize encrypted LocalStorage sandboxes and are retained only as long as necessary to serve operational tracking needs.

Users may clear locally cached batch timelines at any time using the "Reset Demo Data" control located in the role selection interface.

6. Rights of Data Principals (Farmers & Users)

In compliance with Section 11 of the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, you possess the right to:

  • Right to Access: View all active batch records and directory profiles linked to your identifier.
  • Right to Correction: Update outdated quality grades, remaining shelf-life days, or business contact information.
  • Right to Erasure: Request permanent deletion of batch records and grievance filings.
  • Right of Grievance Redressal: File a formal inquiry regarding suspected data mishandling.

7. Grievance Redressal Officer

For inquiries, data correction requests, or privacy concerns, contact our designated Data Protection Officer:

Officer: Grievance Redressal Cell, KrishiSetu

Response Turnaround: Within 48 business hours as mandated by Indian IT guidelines.
